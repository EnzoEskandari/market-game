Equity and fund prices include reinvested dividends. Rates, yields, inflation and grain prices come from Federal Reserve (FRED) and BLS series; gold is the LBMA monthly price; the index fund tracks the S&P 500 with dividends reinvested. Company names are fictional, but each one's price history is a real listed company from that period — revealed when the run ends. Share prices are rescaled to lifelike opening values; every percentage move is the real one.
